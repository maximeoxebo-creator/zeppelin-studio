Support

Tell us what you’re building.

Installation, a setting that won’t behave, a section you want to use — write to us. Every message is read by the studio and answered by the people who wrote the theme.

For example: yourstore.myshopify.com
A screenshot of the problem helps us answer faster. Up to 10 MB.
We answer within one to two business days.

By email

contact.zeppelin.studio@gmail.com

Response time

One to two business days, Monday to Friday. A broken live store or an unreachable checkout is handled first.

What we cover

Bugs in the theme, how a setting or a section works, and help with this documentation. We don’t cover custom development, third-party apps, or issues coming from Shopify itself — we’ll tell you plainly and point you to the right place.

Before you edit

Duplicate your theme before changing its code or reworking its settings: Online Store > Themes > … > Duplicate.

Custom work

For changes beyond the theme’s settings, we recommend a Shopify Partner. Support is offered in English and French.

Quick answers

Many questions are answered in the FAQ and the documentation.
