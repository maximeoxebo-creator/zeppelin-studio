Theme settings

Product cards

Turn on the quick view and quick add buttons shown on product cards.

Open these settings#

  1. In the theme editor, click the Theme settings icon (the paintbrush) in the left toolbar.
  2. Click Product cards to expand the group.
  3. Changes preview instantly. Click Save to publish them.

Both buttons appear on every product card of the store: collection grids, featured collections, the scrolling product row, recommendations and search results. See the full guide: Quick view & quick add.

Settings#

General

Quick view
Toggle

A glass button on each product image opens a side panel with the photos, price, options and add to cart.

Default: On
Quick add
Toggle

A glass + button adds the product to the cart. Products with options open the side panel to choose them first.

Default: On
