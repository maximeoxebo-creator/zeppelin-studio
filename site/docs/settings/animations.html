Theme settings

Animations

Turn the reveal of sections on scroll on or off. Visitors who ask their device to reduce motion never see the movement, whatever you choose here.

Open these settings#

  1. In the theme editor, click the Theme settings icon (the paintbrush) in the left toolbar.
  2. Click Animations to expand the group.
  3. Changes preview instantly. Click Save to publish them.

Settings#

General

Reveal on scroll
Toggle

Turn on or off.

Default: On
