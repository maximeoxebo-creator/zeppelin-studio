Liquid glass

Liquid glass is Opaline’s signature: translucent, blurred panels with light edges, laid over photography.

Where you will find it#

The header on scroll, the mega menu, the quick view panel and the card buttons, the product thumbnails, the promotional popup, the footer panel, and many sections: Colorways, Stacked chapters, Duo panel, Editorial grid, Product mosaic, Closing statement, Statement with images, Testimonials, Timeline and Scrolling text.

Sections where you can switch it#

SectionSetting
HeaderFrosted glass effect (liquid glass)
Editorial gridLiquid glass effect (labels + chips)
Product mosaicLiquid glass effect (promo block), with Panel shape and text colors
FooterGlass tone, Glass tint and Glass blur
Age verifierFrosted glass effect
Image / video bannerBackground behind text: glass box, with Glass opacity and Glass blur

Elsewhere the glass is part of the design: what you control is the photograph behind it and the text color on top.

Getting a clean effect#

  • Glass needs something to blur. Photographs with depth and color read best; a flat, near-white image gives the effect nothing to work with.
  • Check the text against the photograph, not against the panel: a light photograph needs dark text, a dark one light text.
  • Panels that carry text use a denser glass, so prices and options stay readable on any background.
i
Good to know

Visitors who ask their device to reduce transparency see solid panels instead of glass, automatically.
