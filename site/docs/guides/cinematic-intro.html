Guide

Cinematic intro

Open your store with a short logo animation: your logo appears over frosted glass, a photograph or a solid color, then gives way to the page.

Turn it on#

  1. In the theme editor, click Header in the sidebar.
  2. Scroll to Cinematic intro and turn on Enable the cinematic intro.
  3. Choose the Intro backdrop: frosted glass over the page, a photograph, or a solid color.
  4. Set the Logo size, Logo color and how long it Holds, then decide when to Show it.
  5. Click Save.

Good to know#

i
Good to know

The intro uses the logo of the header. Set it under Header > Logo header first; a transparent PNG or SVG works best.

✦
Tip

Visitors who ask their device to reduce motion skip the intro. Showing it once per session keeps returning visitors from waiting on every page.
