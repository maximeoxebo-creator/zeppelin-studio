Quick view & quick add

Let visitors look at a product and add it to their cart from any product card, without leaving the page.

Turn them on or off#

  1. In the theme editor, open Theme settings > Product cards.
  2. Use Quick view and Quick add. Both are on by default.

The buttons appear on every product card: collection pages, featured collections, the scrolling product row, product recommendations and search results.

What the + button does#

The product…Clicking +
has a single variantAdds it to the cart straight away. The + turns into a spinner, then a check mark, and the cart drawer opens.
has options (color, size…)Opens the quick view panel on Select options.
requires a subscription, or is a gift cardOpens the quick view panel, which leads to the product page for the details these need.
is sold outNo + button is shown.

The quick view panel#

The panel is a pane of liquid glass with the page blurred behind it. It shows a summary of the product page:

  • a strip of square photos to swipe;
  • the title, price and tax mention, and the start of the description;
  • the same color swatches, quantity selector, subscription options and Add to cart button as the product page;
  • a View full details link to the product page.

Choosing a color updates the price and brings that color’s photo into view. The panel respects the stock limit and shows Shopify’s messages when an item can’t be added. After a successful add, the panel closes and the cart drawer opens.

i
Good to know

The panel loads only when a visitor opens it, so it adds nothing to the page’s loading time. It closes with Esc, keeps the keyboard inside while open, and returns focus to the card afterwards.
