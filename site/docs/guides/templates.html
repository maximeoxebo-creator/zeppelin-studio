Alternate templates

Give one product or one collection its own page layout without changing the others.

Product templates#

The default product template drives every product. Opaline also includes alternate product templates, each fully independent:

TemplateWhat it’s for
technicalDimensioned drawings and compatible uses, like a specification sheet.
compareA before/after slider, for comparisons of finishes or results.
storyA narrative sequence about how the piece is made.
craftMaterials and process, with a statement and workshop imagery.
chaptersFull-screen chapters that stack as visitors scroll.
lookbookFloating editorial images and a colorway switcher.
editorialMore room for story-driven content.
launchA promotional banner and reassurance, for a dated release.
bundleCross-selling: a row of companion pieces and recommendations.
giftA gifting angle with promotional tiles and reassurance.
trustReviews, press and an FAQ before the footer.
minimalA pared-back layout for simple products.

Collection templates#

TemplateGrid
wide3 columns, portrait cards, names aligned left.
gallery4 columns, portrait, no gap: the photography reads as one surface.
compact4 columns, square cards close together.
minimal4 columns, image and name only, prices hidden.
editorial2 columns, large cards framed like a magazine spread.
showcase2 columns with generous space, for a small range.
dense2 columns of large square cards.
list2 columns of landscape cards, 1 on mobile.

Assign a template#

  1. In the Shopify admin, open the product (or the collection).
  2. In the right sidebar, find Theme template.
  3. Choose a template and save.
  4. In the theme editor, pick that template in the page selector to edit it. Changes apply to every product or collection using it.

Create your own#

In the theme editor, open the page selector, choose Products or Collections, then Create template. Base it on an existing template and give it a name.

i
Good to know

A template shows nothing until a product or collection is assigned to it.
