What’s new

The changes in each version of Opaline.

1.3.1#

  • Quick view and quick add on every product card, in liquid glass, with two new settings under Theme settings > Product cards.
  • Product page gallery: thumbnails and a media aspect ratio for the stacked layout, and margins around media for both layouts.
  • Stock limit: the quantity stops at the available stock, with a message, and the cart refreshes after a refused add.
  • Videos: only one product video plays at a time, including YouTube and Vimeo.
  • Featured product: styled add-to-cart button, video frames, and options as pills.
  • Statement with images: product photos sized to the text, tighter line spacing, captions under each product.
  • Technical drawings: tabs fill on hover. Manifesto: italic accents no longer break across lines.
  • Accessibility: text contrast checked across every page; translated messages display apostrophes correctly.
  • The draggable product row is now called Scrolling product row, and the popup counts in days.

1.3.0#

  • The variant SKU can be shown on the product page and the featured product.
  • Unavailable option values are crossed out in the variant picker.
  • Accelerated checkout buttons are hidden on sold-out variants.
  • Gift cards require a recipient email when sent to someone else.
