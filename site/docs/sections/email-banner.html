Forms & utilities

Email banner

The sign-up banner of your password page, shown while your store is closed to the public.

12 section settings3 block types · 4 settingsAvailable on the password template only

Add the section#

Available on the password template only.

  1. From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store > Themes, then click Customize next to Opaline.
  2. Use the page selector at the top of the editor to open the template you want to change.
  3. In the sidebar, click Add section where you want it to appear.
  4. Select Email banner. Click the section in the sidebar to open its settings.
  5. Click Save in the top right corner when you are done.
