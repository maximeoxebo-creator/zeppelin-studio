Products & collections

Featured collection

Products from one collection in a grid or a carousel, with an optional View all button.

25 section settingsAvailable on every template. It cannot be placed in the header or footer

Add the section#

Available on every template. It cannot be placed in the header or footer.

  1. From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store > Themes, then click Customize next to Opaline.
  2. Use the page selector at the top of the editor to open the template you want to change.
  3. In the sidebar, click Add section where you want it to appear.
  4. Select Featured collection. Click the section in the sidebar to open its settings.
  5. Click Save in the top right corner when you are done.
