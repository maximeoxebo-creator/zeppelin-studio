Products & collections

Product recommendations

Related or complementary products chosen automatically by Shopify's product recommendations.

Add the section#

Available on the product template only.

  1. From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store > Themes, then click Customize next to Opaline.
  2. Use the page selector at the top of the editor to open the template you want to change.
  3. In the sidebar, click Add section where you want it to appear.
  4. Select Product recommendations. Click the section in the sidebar to open its settings.
  5. Click Save in the top right corner when you are done.

Good to know#

i
Good to know

Recommendations are calculated by Shopify from your sales and product descriptions. A new store may show few or none at first. You can choose them yourself with Shopify's free Search & Discovery app.
