Header, footer & overlays

Promotional popup

A promotional glass window that opens after a delay. It can hand over a discount code to copy or collect an email address. Visitors who close it don't see it again for the number of days you choose.

16 section settingsAvailable on every template

Add the section#

Available on every template.

  1. From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store > Themes, then click Customize next to Opaline.
  2. Use the page selector at the top of the editor to open the template you want to change.
  3. In the sidebar, click Add section where you want it to appear.
  4. Select Promotional popup. Click the section in the sidebar to open its settings.
  5. Click Save in the top right corner when you are done.
