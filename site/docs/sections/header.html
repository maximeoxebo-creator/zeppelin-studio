Header, footer & overlays

Header

Your logo, main menu, search, account and cart. On the home page the header can sit transparent over the opening image and turn into frosted glass as visitors scroll. It can also open the site with a short cinematic logo intro.

35 section settingsAvailable on every template

Find the section#

It lives in the Header area at the top of the sidebar and appears on every page.
