Heroes & banners

Image / video banner

A full-width image or video with an eyebrow, heading, text and buttons. The text can sit on the image, on an overlay, or inside a frosted glass box, with optional parallax.

39 section settingsAvailable on every template

Add the section#

Available on every template.

  1. From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store > Themes, then click Customize next to Opaline.
  2. Use the page selector at the top of the editor to open the template you want to change.
  3. In the sidebar, click Add section where you want it to appear.
  4. Select Image / video banner. Click the section in the sidebar to open its settings.
  5. Click Save in the top right corner when you are done.
