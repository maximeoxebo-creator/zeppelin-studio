Products & collections

Scrolling product row

A horizontal row of products that visitors scroll with a swipe or a drag. It can show collection tabs, and a custom cursor label follows the mouse.

17 section settings1 block type · 2 settingsAvailable on every template

Add the section#

Available on every template.

  1. From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store > Themes, then click Customize next to Opaline.
  2. Use the page selector at the top of the editor to open the template you want to change.
  3. In the sidebar, click Add section where you want it to appear.
  4. Select Scrolling product row. Click the section in the sidebar to open its settings.
  5. Click Save in the top right corner when you are done.
