Header, footer & overlays

Footer

Newsletter sign-up, reassurance messages, link columns, social icons, payment icons, policy links and the country and language selectors. It can sit on a photograph behind a frosted glass panel.

Find the section#

It lives in the Footer area at the bottom of the sidebar and appears on every page.
