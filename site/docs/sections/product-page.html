Product page

The product page: media gallery, title, price, options, subscriptions, quantity, add to cart, description, accordion panels and complementary products.

21 section settings10 block types · 8 settingsAccepts app blocksAvailable on the product template only

Add the section#

Available on the product template only.

  1. From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store > Themes, then click Customize next to Opaline.
  2. Use the page selector at the top of the editor to open the template you want to change.
  3. In the sidebar, click Add section where you want it to appear.
  4. Select Product page. Click the section in the sidebar to open its settings.
  5. Click Save in the top right corner when you are done.
