Get started

Before you go live

A short checklist to open your store with every page in order.

Content#

  • Replace every placeholder image with your own photographs. Product photos read best in portrait (4:5 or 3:4).
  • Add alternative text to your images in the Shopify admin. Opaline uses it everywhere the image appears.
  • Fill in the Logo and Favicon under Theme settings > Logo.
  • Check the main menu and footer menus under Content > Menus.

Shopping#

  • Open a product with several colors and check that the gallery follows each color (see Color variants).
  • Add a product to the cart from a product page, from a collection with Quick add, and from the cart page.
  • Try a product with low stock: the quantity stops at what is left, with a message.
  • Check the cart drawer and the checkout button.

Store-wide#

  • Preview every template on mobile.
  • Check the text contrast of your color schemes on photographs.
  • Turn on the languages you sell in under Settings > Languages.
  • Fill in the policies under Settings > Policies; the footer links to them.
