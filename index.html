Signature effect

Liquid glass, done right

Translucent panels that blur whatever sits behind them, used throughout the theme rather than shown off in a corner. The panels below are the real effect, rendered live.

Header · Cart · Popup · Comparator · Countdown · Product mosaic

A material, not a section

Glass isn’t a set of demo blocks bolted on the side — it runs through the theme. The sticky header, the cart drawer, the promotional popup, the before/after handle, the countdown dial, the mosaic’s promo card: each one is a translucent panel that blurs whatever passes behind it.

Only where it has something to refract

  • Over imagery Glass is applied where a surface rests on a photograph or on scrolling content — never over a flat fill, where it reads as grey paper.
  • Low tint, wide blur The clarity comes from the filter, not from an opaque background. The page stays readable through the panel.
  • Specular edges A bright inner top edge and a soft inner shadow give the pane its thickness.
  • Optional Countdown, mosaic, popup and header each carry a switch — glass, hairline, or nothing at all.

Legible & accessible

Dark text on light glass is checked for contrast against the worst case — a night photograph behind the panel. And when a visitor’s device asks for reduced transparency, the blur is dropped for a near-solid surface, so nothing ever becomes hard to read.

On arrival

A cinematic opening

The homepage can open on a full screen: your logo held large over a blurred field, then the blur lifts and the logo travels into its place in the header. The page appears to come into focus rather than simply load. A click, a tap or any key skips it, and it can be shown once per session rather than on every visit.
