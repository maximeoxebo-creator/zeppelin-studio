Installation

  1. In the Shopify admin, open Online Store → Themes.
  2. Click Add theme → Upload zip file and select the theme .zip.
  3. Once imported, click Publish to make it live, or Customize to edit it first.
The message “Updates are not supported for themes uploaded manually” is normal for any manually installed theme. It does not affect how the theme works.

Customization basics

Everything is configured in the Shopify theme editor — no code required.

Colours & typography

Under Theme settings, adjust colour schemes, heading and body fonts, and the type scale. Opaline ships with Fraunces (headings) and DM Sans (body), both served from the Shopify font library.

Header & footer

Logo, navigation menu, icons (search, account, cart), and footer blocks are set in their respective section groups. The header exposes its own padding controls for desktop and mobile separately.

The editor caches section schemas per session. After a theme update, fully close and reopen the editor (or hard-refresh with Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + R) so new settings appear.

Cinematic hero Advanced

The cinematic hero is the theme’s opening statement: a full-height image or video with the header laid transparently over it, so the media runs edge to edge from the very top of the page.

Media

Set an Image, and optionally a Mobile image — a portrait crop of the same scene, so a wide cinematic frame doesn’t become a thin strip on a phone. A Video replaces the image when set.

Autoplaying video is costly on a phone and some browsers refuse it outright, so Play the video on mobile is off by default. Leave it off and the mobile image is used instead.

Framing and atmosphere

SettingWhat it does
HeightHow much of the screen the hero fills.
DarkeningA scrim over the media, so light photography can still carry white text.
Slow zoomHow far the media drifts while the hero is on screen. Zero holds it still.
Film grain overlayA fine grain over the media, for a photographic rather than digital feel.
AlignmentWhere the headline block sits within the frame.

Content

Beyond the overline, heading, text and two buttons, the hero carries three optional extras:

  • A product capsule — pick a product and it appears as a small card over the media, with its own label, title and link.
  • A scroll indicator with its own label, hinting that the page continues.
  • Corner text, left and right — for a season, a place, a collection number.
The header sits transparent over the hero, then takes on its own background once the visitor scrolls past it, so navigation stays legible over the content underneath. The same behaviour applies to any full-bleed section, such as the Colourways stage.

Liquid-glass sections Advanced

Liquid glass is a system rather than a single section: a translucent, blurred panel with specular edges, layered over photography. It appears in Colourways, Stacked chapters, Duo panel, Editorial grid, Product mosaic, Closing statement, Inlaid statement, Testimonials, Timeline, Scrolling text, Promotional popup and the footer newsletter.

Where you can switch it on and off

SectionSetting
Editorial gridLiquid glass effect (labels + chips) — with it off, labels sit directly on the image.
Product mosaicLiquid glass effect (promo block), plus Panel shape (rounded or square) and separate Panel text, Title and Subtitle colours.
Scrolling textLiquid glass effect on the moving band.

In the other sections the glass is part of the design and needs no setting — what you control is the media behind it and the text colour on top.

The theme does not ship its own cookie banner — Shopify provides one under Settings → Customer privacy. What the theme adds is Frosted glass on the cookie banner in Theme settings, which dresses Shopify’s banner to match the rest of the glass.

Getting a clean glass effect

  • Busy, high-contrast photographs read best through frosted glass. A flat, near-white image gives the panel nothing to refract.
  • Watch the text colour against the photograph you chose, not against the panel — a light photograph needs dark text on the glass, a dark one needs light text.
  • The blur is applied automatically; there is no opacity slider to tune. The panels are calibrated to stay legible over both light and dark imagery.

Colourways Advanced

Colourways shows one range in several finishes. The visitor clicks a swatch and the photograph, the background wash and the glass panel change together.

Setting it up

  1. Add a Colourway block for each finish you carry — up to six.
  2. Give each block its Image (the whole range in that finish reads better than a single piece), a Name and a short Note.
  3. Set the Swatch colour — this is the chip the visitor clicks.
  4. Set the Background wash — the section’s background tints towards this colour when that finish is active. A pale version of the swatch works best.

Framing

Visual size offers three settings: Contained keeps the page gutter, Edge to edge and Full screen pull the stage out to the full width of the window. The two wider settings use Visual height instead of a fixed ratio, and it is capped on small screens so the swatches stay reachable without scrolling.

The first block is the one shown when the page loads. Put your signature finish first.

Product colour variants Advanced

The product page reacts live when a customer switches colour, with no page reload. Changing the swatch updates the gallery and the price in place.

How it works

  • Colour swatches — turn on Enable swatches in the product section. Each variant’s colour becomes a clickable chip instead of a dropdown, and Swatch shape sets whether the chips are round or square.
  • Per-colour media filtering — turn on Filter media by variant, then associate each product image with a colour in Shopify. When a colour is selected the gallery shows only that colour’s media; images with no colour assigned always stay visible.

Setup checklist

  1. In the product admin, add a Colour (or equivalent) option with your variants.
  2. Upload the product images, then use Shopify’s media-by-variant association to attach each image to a colour.
  3. In the theme editor, enable swatches and media filtering on the product section.
  4. Preview the product and click through each colour to confirm the gallery follows.

Alternate product pages Advanced

Opaline includes ready-made alternate product templates so different products can have a completely independent layout and content.

Why use them

The default product template drives every product. When you want one product (or a family) to have its own overline, sections and collapsible tabs — without affecting the rest of the catalog — assign it an alternate template instead of editing the shared one.

How to assign a template to a product

  1. Open the product in Products in the Shopify admin.
  2. In the right sidebar, find the Theme template selector.
  3. Choose an alternate template (for example technical, compare or lookbook) and save.
  4. Open that product in the theme editor — you’re now editing only that template. Its content is unique to the products using it.
Every alternate template is fully independent. Editing one never changes another, so each product family can have its own bespoke page while the rest of the catalog keeps the default layout.

  1. Open the collection under Products → Collections in the Shopify admin.
  2. In the right sidebar, find the Theme template selector.
  3. Choose an alternate template (for example gallery or editorial) and save.
The sections below the grid are the same across every collection template, so switching template changes how products are laid out — not the story the page tells underneath. Reorder or replace those sections per template if you want them to differ too.

Promotional tiles inside the grid

The product grid accepts two kinds of block that sit among the products rather than after them:

  • Wide product — pick a product and a position, and its card spans two columns instead of one.
  • Promo — an image or colour tile with its own overline, heading and button, placed at a chosen position and spanning one or two columns.

Both take a position number, counted in grid cells, so you can drop a feature after the third product and another halfway down the page.

Mobile text & button sizes

Several sections expose separate mobile size controls, so small screens can be tuned without touching the desktop composition:

  • Image / video banner — overline, heading, text and button scale, each with a mobile counterpart, plus a dedicated mobile image.
  • Product grid — columns, overline and title sizes, category-navigation size, and whether breadcrumbs show at all.
  • Scrolling text — its own mobile font size.
  • Multiple columns — mobile column count, and whether the columns become a swipeable rail.
  • Header — padding above, below and inside, set separately for mobile.

Mobile sizes work as a multiplier on top of the desktop size: 100% means “same as desktop,” higher enlarges, lower shrinks — for mobile only. The desktop size always applies first, so you only touch the mobile control when small screens need a tweak.

Title vs. body colours

Sections with both a heading and running text expose separate colour settings rather than one blanket colour. Before / After is the fullest example, with independent background, overline, title and text colours.

  • Title colour applies only to the heading.
  • Body / text colour applies only to the running text.
  • Overline colour is independent and defaults to your accent colour.

Leaving a colour empty keeps the theme’s default for that element, which follows the section’s colour scheme.

Languages

Storefront text ships in five languages: English (default), French, German, Spanish and Italian. Every key is translated in all five — buttons, cart, search, forms, error messages.

The theme editor’s own labels are translated into English and French. Enable a language under Settings → Languages in the Shopify admin; the theme follows automatically.

Motion & accessibility

Motion in Opaline is tied to scroll position rather than to timers, so nothing plays behind the visitor’s back. Headlines are split into lines that rise into place, sections reveal as they enter view, and some visuals drift as they pass.

Visitors who ask their system to reduce motion get the same content with the movement removed — everything appears in place. This is honoured across the theme and needs no setting.

Every image carries alternative text, falling back to a label from the block and then to your shop name when you haven’t set one in Shopify. Each page carries exactly one level-one heading.

Best practices

  • Use images at the right ratio (portrait 3:4 for products) for crisp rendering.
  • Prefer photography with depth behind glass panels — flat, near-white images give the glass nothing to work with.
  • Always test on mobile after changing a layout, especially sections with independent mobile positions.
  • Your saved content is safe across theme updates — settings and uploaded images persist.
